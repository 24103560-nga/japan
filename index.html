Welcome

Japanese cuisine is famous for fresh seasonal ingredients, balanced flavors and beautiful presentation. In 2013, traditional Japanese cuisine, called washoku, was added to UNESCO's list of Intangible Cultural Heritage.

Whether you love a comforting bowl of noodles or a delicate piece of sushi, this site will help you discover some of the most popular foods in Japan.

Featured Favorites

Sushi

Vinegared rice paired with fresh fish, egg or vegetables, served as nigiri or rolled into maki.

Ramen

Wheat noodles in a rich broth. Popular styles include shoyu, miso, shio and tonkotsu.

Matcha

Finely ground green tea powder whisked with hot water into a smooth, slightly bitter drink.

Dango

Chewy rice-flour dumplings on a skewer, often enjoyed with tea.
