A Sweet Ending

Traditional Japanese sweets are called wagashi. They are usually less sugary than Western desserts and are often enjoyed together with a cup of green tea.

Tea and Treats

Matcha

Matcha is a powdered green tea used in the traditional tea ceremony and in many modern desserts.

Dango

Dango come in many styles, such as three-colored hanami dango and mitarashi dango with a sweet soy glaze.

How to Whisk Matcha

  1. Sift about two scoops of matcha powder into a warm bowl.
  2. Pour in a small amount of hot, not boiling, water.
  3. Whisk quickly in a zigzag motion until a fine foam forms.
